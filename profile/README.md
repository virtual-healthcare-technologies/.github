# VHT.ai

**Virtual Healthcare Technologies Inc.** builds VHT.ai, a member health app that brings your everyday health data together in one place and helps you act on it.

> VHT.ai is in beta. It provides educational guidance only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you think you may have a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services.

## What the app does

- **Health Index** — one score built from six pillars of your everyday health data, so you can see where you are today and how things are trending.
- **ECHNA** — an AI health companion that explains your data in plain language, suggests small goals, and nudges with empathy rather than pressure.
- **Your records, your devices** — lab results, vitals, medications, wearables and daily logs, unified in one member portal.
- **7 UI languages** on iOS, Android and the web.
- **HIPAA-aligned** privacy and security practices.

Learn more at **https://vht.ai**.

## One rule above all

**Never post personal health information (PHI)** in issues, discussions or pull requests — not yours, not anyone else's. Describe the problem without the data. If you need to share something sensitive to reproduce a bug, email contact@vht.ai and we will arrange a private channel.
