# VHT.ai — Virtual Healthcare Technologies Inc.

VHT.ai is a member health app that brings your everyday health data together in one place and helps you understand and act on it, with ECHNA, an AI health companion, at the centre.

> **VHT.ai is in beta.** It provides educational guidance only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you think you may have a medical emergency, call your local emergency number.

**Website:** https://vht.ai · **Join the beta:** https://vht.ai/beta · **Contact:** contact@vht.ai

---

## What VHT.ai does

| | |
|---|---|
| **Health Index** | One score built from six pillars of your everyday health data (clinical, biometrics, lifestyle, preventive care, genetics, environment), so you can see where you are today and how things are trending. |
| **ECHNA** | An AI health companion that explains your data in plain language, answers questions, suggests small achievable goals and remembers your context, with empathy rather than pressure. |
| **Your records** | Conditions, medications, allergies, lab results, vaccinations, procedures, family history and lifestyle, in one member profile you control. |
| **Your devices** | Apple Health and Health Connect, smart rings, blood-pressure cuffs, glucose monitors and daily quick-logs. |
| **Digital Twin** | A living model of your health that shows how goals and habits connect. |
| **Languages** | English, French, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, German and Brazilian Portuguese, across the whole app. |
| **Platforms** | iOS, Android and the web. |
| **Privacy** | HIPAA-aligned practices: encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, audit trails, and we never sell your data. See the [Privacy Policy](https://vht.ai/privacy) and [Security](https://vht.ai/security). |

## One rule above all: no personal health information

**Never post personal health information (PHI)** in issues, discussions or pull requests. Not yours, not anyone else's. Describe the problem without the data: "the blood-pressure chart shows the wrong unit", not your readings. Every issue template starts with a checkbox confirming this.

If reproducing a bug needs something sensitive, email contact@vht.ai and we will arrange a private channel. Anything containing health information in a public thread will be edited by a maintainer and labelled `phi-removed`.

## How we work in the open

- **Release notes** for every app update are posted in [Announcements](https://github.com/virtual-healthcare-technologies/community/discussions/categories/announcements).
- **Bugs and requests** are tracked in the public [community issues](https://github.com/virtual-healthcare-technologies/community/issues), labelled `bug`, `feature`, `translation`, `question` and `needs-info`.
- **Security reports** follow coordinated disclosure: we acknowledge within one business day, keep you updated, and credit you if you wish. Good-faith research under the policy is welcome; testing against real member accounts is not.
- **Content licence:** everything in the public repos is [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) unless stated otherwise.
